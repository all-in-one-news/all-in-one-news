New York Mayor Mamdani disbands 'notorious' police unit that used excessive force on protesters

2026-11-17T04:10:00+00:00

Mamdani promised in his election campaign last year to disband the police department's Strategic Response Group, a decade-old anti-terrorism unit that has faced legal challenges for its rough tactics at street protests, including the use of pepper spray by armed officers in riot gear and mass arrests.
