Judo champion Teddy Riner launches bid for French presidency

2026-11-15T04:20:00+00:00

The 37-year-old Guadeloupe-born athlete has no experience in public office, formal backing from a political party or detailed manifesto. He launched his campaign under the slogan "La France qu'on aime" (The France we love), using social media broadcasts and a visit to a hospital in Seine-Saint-Denis to introduce his candidacy
