173 Bangladeshi return from Libya under voluntary repatriation programme

2026-11-26T06:50:00+00:00

According to a press release issued today, they arrived at 4:30am on a Buraq Air flight (UZ-222), with assistance from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Bangladesh Embassy in Libya and the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment.
