Over 3 lakh people in India's Odisha evacuated as Cyclone Akvan moves closer to landfall

2026-11-20T04:00:00+00:00

The Odisha government has set up 9,341 cyclone shelters across potentially affected districts as the storm, the third of its kind to threaten the state in 24 days, shifted its trajectory after initially being projected to make landfall in Andhra Pradesh.
