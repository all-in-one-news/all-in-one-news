While there is an ample supply of fish in the markets, prices remain high. In the markets of Chattogram, the same hilsa is being sold at different prices in different shops. There is also no price control for other types of fish, or even for chicken and meat. Most shops lack a displayed price list, and consumers complain that, in the absence of oversight, sellers are charging whatever prices they please.
