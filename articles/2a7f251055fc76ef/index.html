BGMEA flags data, methodological 'flaws' in CPD’s wage proposal for RMG workers

2026-11-20T17:45:00+00:00

The BGMEA has flagged methodological inconsistencies in CPD’s proposed Tk18,578–Tk24,204 minimum wage range for garment workers, urging the think tank to factor in regional living costs, rising production expenses, and global competitiveness.
