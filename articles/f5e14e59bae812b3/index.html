18 Arab and Islamic nations condemn Israeli strike on Gaza hospital

2026-11-15T05:10:00+00:00

In a joint statement issued on Saturday, the foreign ministers of the 18 countries, including Egypt, Jordan, Türkiye and Qatar, condemned the attack as a “violation of international humanitarian law, which affords special protection to medical facilities and healthcare workers”
