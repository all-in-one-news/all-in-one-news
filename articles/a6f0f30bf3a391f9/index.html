Rights groups demand sanctions on Myanmar after UN court rules Rohingya genocide took place

2026-11-21T04:15:00+00:00

The International Court of Justice (ICJ), in its first ruling of this nature, ordered Myanmar's military-backed government to ensure the safe return of Rohingya refugees who fled the violence, provide compensation to victims and bring those suspected of genocide to justice.
