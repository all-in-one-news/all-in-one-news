PM's Adviser Swapan seeks stronger UNDP, UNFPA support for Bangladesh's development priorities

2026-11-17T03:55:00+00:00

During his meeting with UNDP, Zahir Uddin Swapan highlighted the need for technical assistance in implementing Bangladesh’s Smooth Transition Strategy (STS), strengthening institutional and regulatory capacities, improving the business environment and attracting foreign direct investment.
