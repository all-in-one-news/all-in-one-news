Bangladesh's EU denim market share shrinks despite retaining top spot

2026-11-19T15:45:00+00:00

Imports from Bangladesh declined 5.48% to €1.01b, bringing its share of the EU denim market down to 29.48%, from 30.18% in the corresponding period last year.
