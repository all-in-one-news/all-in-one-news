Minimum wage for garment workers set at Tk17,000 through vote

2026-11-26T09:20:00+00:00

For the first time, the Minimum Wage Board has set a new minimum wage through a vote after just three years, instead of the usual five, with four of its six members voting in favour of Tk17,000.
