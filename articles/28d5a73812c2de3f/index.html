Khosru briefs parliament on new pathway towards LDC graduation by 2029

2026-11-17T03:50:00+00:00

Responding to a call-attention notice from reserved-seat MP Sultana Jasmine in parliament, he said Bangladesh was originally scheduled to graduate on 24 November 2026. However, post-Covid economic pressures, the Russia-Ukraine war, the Middle East conflict, political changes in July 2024 and financial-sector problems disrupted preparations and reforms.
