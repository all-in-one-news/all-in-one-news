The incident occurred at dawn in Satpar Paschimpara village, located in the Sadar Upazila. The deceased, Babul Biswas, was a resident of Kashiani Upazila. According to the police, Babul broke into the home of a villager named Swapan Biswas late at night to commit theft. When the family members realized what was happening and raised an alarm, local residents rushed to the scene.
