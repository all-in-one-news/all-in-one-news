5 killed in bus-CNG auto-rickshaw collision in Cox's Bazar

2026-11-25T05:15:00+00:00

According to the Malumghat Highway Police Outpost, a Cox’s Bazar-bound Hanif Paribahan bus, registration number Dhaka Metro-B-11-9975, collided head-on with a CNG-run auto-rickshaw, registration number Cox’s Bazar-Tha-11-1920, coming from the opposite direction.
