Bangladesh welcomes ICJ genocide ruling, seeks global pressure for Rohingya repatriation

2026-11-19T14:20:00+00:00

State ministers Humaiun Kobir and Shama Obaed Islam say the ICJ judgment strengthens Bangladesh's diplomatic position and could help increase international pressure on Myanmar and the Arakan Army to ensure the safe and dignified repatriation of Rohingya refugees.
